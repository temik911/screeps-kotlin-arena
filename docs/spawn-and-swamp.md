# Spawn and Swamp (season 4) — the bot's rules, history and tooling

The bot names itself in the first line of every match log — `hello season4 spawn-and-swamp vN` plus a `tuning:` line with the
thresholds that decided that match. `BOT_VERSION` in `SpawnAndSwamp.kt` is that N; it goes up with every behaviour change that
reaches a live match, and the landing tag `spawn-and-swamp-vN` matches it.

Owner: the Spawn and Swamp session. Code: `starter/src/jsMain/kotlin/season4/spawnandswamp/`. Only this bot's session edits this file (see the parallel-sessions rules in `CLAUDE.md`).

## Measured rules and design

**`season4/spawnandswamp/`** — Season 4 "Spawn and Swamp" (basic) bot. Measured rules (02.09.2026): spawn starts with 1000 energy, regen 1/tick; permanent 2500-energy containers in the map corners, plus a pair of temporary 2000-energy containers (99-tick decay) appearing every 50 ticks anywhere; ~1/3 swamp, ~1/3 walls, spawns sit in wall pockets so **path distance, never Chebyshev**. **Breach first**: each spawn has a 5000-energy container locked behind `StructureWall`s (10000 hits, attackable) nine cells away — `breachPlan` runs Dijkstra treating structure walls as passable at a wall-count-first cost, a melee `[MOVE,ATTACK]×k` breacher (k ≈ √(hits/180), spawn+break time minimum) is spawned before anything else, idle home fighters shoot the current wall, and `DistanceMap.syncWalls` drops the static wall cache when a wall dies (the stub run: wall down at t≈95, income 4→25/tick, enemy spawn dead at t=391 instead of 648). Economy: hauler fleet sized from the income the spawn can convert into fighters (`targetIncome` = full fighter cost / spawn ticks) vs the projected income of the fleet over the nearest sites (`fleetTrip`); site choice by energy-per-trip with in-flight accounting and decay checks; energy split hauler/fighter is hedged 1:1 after the initial 1000 (`HAULER_LEAD`) — the opening 1000 goes entirely to haulers (a lost match on 02.09 showed the opponent doing exactly that and out-hauling a 500-hauler + 500-guard opening 2:1); a visible enemy army overrides (fighter first, no waiting for bigger bodies while enemy fighters exist). Army: fighter body = the (RANGED blocks, TOUGH+MOVE pairs) pair maximizing damage×HP at 1:1 MOVE (T3 M7 R4 at 1000; no HEAL), and **only full bodies** while income flows (a second lost match: thirteen 300-cost bodies vs three M5R5 lost on equal energy — mass attack punishes swarms). DEFEND post at the spawn; enemies inside the spawn alarm radius are fought by **everyone** regardless of ratio (nine fighters once watched three die from the post); sorties away from home only on **local** superiority (allies within 8 vs enemies within 11, ratio 1.3), then close to range 2 with the damage penalty dropped (the first lost fight: 130 dps vs 80 and lost because fighters sat at range 4-5 while the front one was focused); while a stronger enemy camps the spawn, accumulate to a full body instead of feeding it; body parts are interleaved MOVE/RANGED after TOUGH (a front-loaded MOVE block left crippled 4-RANGED turrets that blocked three waves in a corridor), creeps with no working MOVE are obstacles for pathing and never request moves, the melee breacher stays home and its power is kite-discounted for push decisions, and when the spawn (not energy) is the bottleneck the body is chosen per spawn-tick (M5R5) rather than per energy (T3M7R4); PUSH only against the enemy army **expected at arrival** (current power + observed growth × march time) and in **waves** (a vanguard waits for wave-mates that fall behind by path ticks, never by centroid — a centroid on a wall cell once froze the whole squad), engage blocking enemies on local superiority, "last call" push before the 2000-tick draw. Copies of `InfluenceMap`/`DistanceMap`/`TrafficManager` from spawnstrike (DistanceMap gained a `swampCost` parameter + `stepFieldTo` for empty haulers). `DEBUG_MAP` prints the map, `sites` lines print container appearances, `t=` lines the economy, `enemy creeps` lines (every 50 ticks) enemy bodies as `T10M4R3H1`, `moves:` lines (every 100) the TrafficManager audit (issued moves per kind free/chain/swap, ok/fail by next-tick position), `stuck` lines the first time a creep asks the same step for 8 ticks without moving (who blocks it and what the blocker wants) — so a live run doubles as a probe.

## Version history (one paragraph per live-match iteration; the first six versions are folded into the design paragraph above)

**Seventh version (after a drawn fifth match, 02.09):** *power* is Lanchester — `√(net dps × hits)` per group, enemy heal subtracted from our dps (a T10M4R3H1 turret with 30 dps beats M5R5 with 50 dps 1v1; the old dps-only power said the opposite; the square root keeps the measure linear in numbers so `PUSH_RATIO`/`DEFEND_MARGIN` keep their meaning); *swamp* is penalised in `scoreCell` only without local superiority (a flat 40 exceeded the flow-field gain of 10 at a plain→swamp edge, so a fighter froze five cells from the enemy for 500 ticks with seven fighters queued behind it); *static blockers* (an ally that requested no move last tick, or immobile, or when this creep is already stuck) are detoured around in `bestSingleMove` (a free neighbour at most one swamp step farther), and a stuck hauler paths with all ally cells blocked; *fighter-first* counts only enemies that will *arrive* before we could spawn the deficit (arrival from the observed approach rate over 20 ticks: a stationary guard never arrives; two far scouts kept the spawn on fighters for 170 ticks at income 10/23) and never while we have zero haulers; the *alarm* cancels a push only if the home guard (non-wave fighters) cannot beat the home threats, and marching waves ignore home threats (a scout at the north exit recalled the whole army from the south exit three times); the *melee breacher* is leashed to home threat / breach wall / post (it chased a raider across the map and died as a turret); *hunting* a threat on our half is a group decision with hysteresis and *local aggression* has per-creep hysteresis (two fighters swapped places on the aggression-radius edge for 300 ticks); wave *cohesion* holds while not under fire (was: while no enemy within 5) and counts allies within 4 cells, not 8 (a mate five swamp cells back is 25 ticks away).

**Eighth version (after the sixth match, 02.09 — won at tick ~1292, three fighters lost for nothing):** push/hunt *power* counts only mobile ranged fighters (the breacher's 1200 hits inflated the Lanchester root and sent two wounded fighters as a wave); a *hunt* on our half is decided by the free strikers against the whole enemy pack around the threat (all combat enemies within 11 of it), not against one creep (the guard + breacher "beat" one M3R3 and two hunters walked into three); *waves are staged at home* (free strikers within the spawn's alarm radius by loaded path ticks) so a wave never consists of fighters on different routes; *cohesion hold* also applies to hunters and is released when a mate within engage range is already in combat (one fighter held "for the laggard" three cells from its mate that was dying alone); *rangedMassAttack* is chosen only when combat targets (or the spawn) justify it — enemy haulers streaming past at range 1-2 made mass attack the choice and the duelist at range 3 received 1 damage per part; loaded haulers get a higher traffic priority than empty/parking ones and the idle ring moved from range 2 to 3 (sixteen haulers do not fit sixteen cells, and a delivering hauler was stuck behind parkers).

**Ninth version (after the seventh match, 03.09 — won at tick ~1190, the first wave of two lost for nothing):** the *engine fact that changes everything* — fatigue weight is counted by part TYPE, dead parts included (`movement.js:237`), and only live MOVE parts shed it (`tick.js:105`), so an interleaved M5R5 moves at half speed after its first 100 damage and at a third after 500 (verified in the log: fatigue=4 on plain and 44 on swamp at 676 hits = 5 weights, 3 live MOVEs); `plainPeriod`/`periodAt`/`fullSpeed`/`speedSlack` compute a creep's ticks per cell and the damage it can still take at full speed from its live body; the *fighter body* is chosen by `bodyValue` = dps integrated over the damage the body absorbs while staying full-speed, with the part order derived from the same rule — spare MOVEs first (they die costing neither dps nor speed), then RANGED, then MOVE 1:1 — which gives M8R4 at 1000 (40 dps, 900 hits at full speed; TOUGH is dominated: a TOUGH+MOVE pair costs 60 for 100 hits of slack, a spare MOVE 50 and no weight); *strikers* (wave staging, hunting, push power) are full-speed ranged fighters only, crippled ones stay home as guards, offense power = departed waves + staging while defense power (spawn decisions) counts every armed fighter; *enemy production* replaces "growth": the power of combat creeps first seen within a 300-tick window, over that window (net growth read 0 because we killed the stream as it came, and the posture flipped four times in 300 ticks), expected-at-arrival = enemies not approaching us + production × march, and the wave must separately beat the strongest approaching pack (enemies within 11 of an approaching creep); a *fight cost* (`fightCost`: enemies die weakest-first under our summed dps, everyone still alive keeps shooting) must fit into the group's `speedSlack` for a hunt or a per-creep engagement — Lanchester said an M5R5 "wins" 1v1 vs M3R3, at 450 hits and half its speed; *cohesion* is measured in the mate's real ticks (flow gap × its period), tightens to 2 ticks when a combat enemy is within 11, and any mate in combat releases every hold regardless of distance (one fighter held "for the laggard" 13 cells from a mate dying alone in a swamp); *walls* no longer hide real fire — `damageAt` drops only the approach step behind a wall, and the new `fireAt` (range only) prices a pinned cell in `scoreCell` as extra immobile ticks × incoming fire at any superiority (a fighter held formation for ten ticks under fire from behind a wall; another stepped into swamp at range 2 with a plain cell at range 3 beside it and paid 470 hits for one M3R3). Stub (its fatigue fixed to the same weight rule): freeze 555, none 448, enemy 630 (two guards placed at once read as "production" for 300 ticks — a burst, not a stream), swarm 457, errors 0.

**Tenth version (after the eighth match, 03.09 — won at tick ~1203; the opponent now fields M5R5 `[M×5,R×5]` instead of M3R3, becoming an immobile R-turret after 500 damage):** the *push decision prices the road* — the wave (departed waves + staging) pays `fightCost` for every approaching pack (enemies within 11 of each other fight simultaneously) and for the units the enemy will produce during march + siege (production ÷ typical birth power, each fought alone), keeps one more such fight as a buffer, and departs only if the remaining hits are positive and their Lanchester power beats the enemies *not* approaching us by `PUSH_RATIO` (the old power-vs-power test sent a pair at 438 vs 437 into two M5R5 in a row); *fight decisions are group-consistent* — the cost is compared with the *largest* speed slack among the local allies (or the hunt group), not each creep's own: with per-creep slack a wounded fighter refused and its healthy mate fought an M5R5 alone four cells from it (−950/−330); *weaponless fighters* (all RANGED dead — the M8R4 tail) hold a post outside the delivery ring and flee only from nearby enemies, and never count as home guard; the spawn *waits for the full body* whenever the guard already holds (a runt adds nothing) or the missing energy arrives sooner than the enemy — arrival is computed from loaded haulers by loaded-path ticks, then income (income 0 with 2150 in transit read as 773 ticks and produced M1R1/M2R1); *hauler husks* (all CARRY dead) leave the delivery ring and free their slot in `MAX_HAULERS` (six sat in DELIVER next to the spawn for 400 ticks); a *loaded hauler fleeing into swamp drops its load* (empty it moves 1 cell/tick even on swamp; the pile decays 1/tick and gets picked up later); and *no hauler is spawned while the spawn is full with energy in transit* — `targetIncome` now follows the body actually built at a full spawn (M8R4, 27.7/tick), which alone would have grown the fleet to 15 while the spawn idled full. Stub: freeze 573, none 461, enemy 473, swarm 469, errors 0.

**Eleventh version (after the ninth match, 03.09 — a third opponent: breaches and hauls its own container, masses balls of five melee healers `M5A1H1` plus an `M5A5` spawn guard; the log ends at tick 1001 with five melee adjacent to our spawn while our army stands 44 cells away with `hold=true` and then departs as wave 21 toward the enemy spawn):** *melee is no longer blanket-discounted* — `meleeFactor` gives the 0.1 kite discount only when every opponent is ranged, none is within 2, and the melee unit is slower than each ranged opponent on swamp (`swampPeriod`; **Twelfth version (after the tenth match, 03.09 — a lone `M5A3` raider sent at tick 30 reached our spawn at ~205 and the log ends at 240 with the breacher standing three cells away for forty ticks, `step=stay`):** `scoreCell` treated our own melee like a ranged kiter — the melee-zone penalty (−15 per melee enemy within 2 when aggressive) and the enemy's influence (−9) outweighed the flow gain of a step (10), so the best cell was the current one at range 3; now a melee creep (no RANGED alive) gets no melee-zone penalty, no influence term while aggressive, and its outgoing-fire term counts enemies within reach 1 only. **Thirteenth version (after the eleventh match, 03.09 — lost; a fifth opponent: the M3R3 stream plus `M4H2` healers, and a `StructureTower` built next to its spawn and fed by ten `M1C1` haulers; four M8R4 of two waves died at the enemy spawn with `combat=false flee=false` and no enemy creep in sight, then seven fighters were fed one at a time into a healed pack of three M3R3 + two M4H2 camping 25 cells from our spawn):** the Arena tower is not the World tower — `TOWER_POWER_ATTACK=1000` at `TOWER_OPTIMAL_RANGE=1`, minus 50 per cell to zero at `TOWER_FALLOFF_RANGE=21` (`TOWER_FALLOFF=1`), one shot per `TOWER_COOLDOWN=10` for `TOWER_ENERGY_COST=10` with `TOWER_CAPACITY=10` (one shot per refill, so its ammunition is the haulers standing next to it), 3000 hits, 1250 to build; a single shot at range ≤4 removes an M8R4's four MOVE and four RANGED (every loss in the log is 1000−50×(r−1) to the hit point). The bot saw towers only as obstacles. Now: *towers are fire* — `InfluenceMap` holds the enemy towers per tick (`TowerThreat`: fed = a shot's energy inside or an enemy creep with energy within `TOWER_COOLDOWN` cells), `towerShot(r)` comes from the arena constants (documented fallbacks, read as JS numbers so an undefined or fractional constant can neither NaN nor truncate the arithmetic), `towerSustainedAt` (shot/cooldown) enters `damageAt`/`fireAt`/`influenceAt`/`enemyPressureAt`/`dangerCostMatrix`, `towerBurstAt` (the next shot) drives `mustFlee` for anyone not in an active siege; *the push is a simulation, not a hit pool* — `siegeOutcome` walks the wave down the flow field from its position to range 3 of the enemy spawn in the slowest member's ticks per cell (`periodAt`) with every fed tower firing at the current range once per cooldown at the highest-dps creep, then the siege: defenders (healers first, focusing our weakest) die under our summed dps minus their heal, then the spawn (×1.3 to depart, ×0.9 to continue, plus any rampart on it); damage removes parts front-to-back so one shot zeroes a striker's dps exactly as the engine does (the old pool said four M8R4 "survive" a tower at 90 average dps); the departure test runs on the *staging group alone* (reinforcement pairs used to leave on the combined estimate and arrive one wave at a time) and must survive one extra shot at the ring (`extraShots=1` — a "win with 1400 hits to spare" became a retreat when the second approach shot landed 150 higher than modelled), the continuation test on the departed wave; *no piecemeal defence* — `homeFight` is true only when the spawn is under fire, a home threat is at the gates (within post + range + 1 of the spawn) or the whole home garrison beats all home threats by `DEFEND_MARGIN`; otherwise everyone keeps the post and reinforcements accumulate (match 9's "everyone fights at home" survives in the gates clause), the melee breacher included (it chased a kiting M3R3 for 60 ticks and died without landing a hit); *fightCost* subtracts the heal of enemies still alive and kills healers first, like `healAndShoot` (heal-blind, a pair "took" two M3R3 + M4H2 for 1227 hits and both waves died); *towers change movement* — cohesion gathers at the coverage edge (tight gap near a tower, no hold inside coverage: five held for laggards 14 cells from the tower for 70 ticks losing one per shot), `inCombat` means enemy creeps only (the full cell score under a tower alone gave ally-influence inertia: ten fighters stood at the swamp edge for 120 ticks), the swamp-pinning term excludes tower fire (standing still costs the same), the tower's sustained damage is a tie-breaker in every cell score so the ring forms on the far side, an armed striker in an active siege does not flee the next shot (its death is already priced in and it soaks a shot a healthy one would take), and `shoot` targets the spawn under storm only while no combat creep is in range (haulers feeding the tower must not draw mass attacks); *unseen damage* — each fighter remembers its hits and cell; a loss that neither creep fire nor a tower burst explains is logged as `ghost damage`, counts as under fire and forces a retreat when one more such hit would kill; *diagnostics* — `tower:` at hello prints the constants and the model's shots at 1..4/10/20, `enemy structures` (every 50 ticks) prints towers with energy/cooldown/fed, enemy ramparts and construction sites with progress. Stub: `tower` scenario (a tower adjacent to the enemy spawn with four M1C1 feeders drawing from the enemy spawn) won at 909 by a single wave of six, `tower+enemy` at 946 by seven; none 461, enemy 508 and ball 1139 (heal-aware cost waits for one more fighter), swamp swarm 469, raider 524, freeze 573, errors 0.

**Sixteenth version (after the fourteenth match, 03.09)**: lost to an M5R1 rusher (two from tick 1, a third at ~200; they parked within three cells of our spawn and never kited) on the same map as matches 12–13.
